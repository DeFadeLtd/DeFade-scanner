# Security

If you find a security problem in DeFade (defade.org, api.defade.org, the iOS and Android apps, the Chrome extension, the Telegram bot or the MCP connector), please tell us privately at **info@defade.org**, with "Security" in the subject.

Please **don't** open a public issue for it.

**What helps us most:**

- what you found, and where;
- the steps to reproduce it;
- what an attacker could do with it.

**While you look:**

- only use accounts and API keys that are your own;
- don't access, change or delete other people's data;
- don't run anything that degrades the service for other users, such as load or denial-of-service tests.

We will acknowledge your report, keep you updated while we fix it, and credit you when it ships if you'd like.

DeFade never asks for your seed phrase or private key, and a scan never needs your wallet. Anyone who asks for either in DeFade's name is not us.
